About Us

A Shield of Protection, A Haven of Opportunity

Aegis Haven Foundation is non-profit organization established to advance the protection, dignity, and inclusion of vulnerable populations, with a primary focus on children and persons with disabilities.

The Foundation is dedicated to addressing critical child protection concerns, including gender-based violence (GBV), particularly where it intersects with the safety and well-being of children.

Founded on a deep-rooted passion for equity and opportunity, the organization reflects the founder's enduring commitment to ensuring that every child regardless of background, ability, or circumstance is given a fair chance to grow, thrive, and realize their full potential.

Why We Exist

Background & Rationale

The challenge that drives our purpose

Children and persons with disabilities often face heightened risks of abuse, neglect, exploitation, and exclusion. Gender-based violence remains a pervasive challenge, disproportionately affecting vulnerable groups and undermining their rights, safety, and development.

Despite increasing awareness, gaps persist in prevention, response mechanisms, and access to essential services. Many communities lack the resources, knowledge, and systems necessary to adequately protect and support those most at risk.

Aegis Haven Foundation was established to serve as a protective shield ("Aegis") and a place of safety ("Haven") for children - a response to these challenges to serve as both a protective force and a source of opportunity. The organization seeks to bridge systemic gaps through integrated, inclusive, and sustainable interventions that prioritize protection, empowerment, and social inclusion.

The Foundation adopts a rights-based, inclusive, and multi-sectoral approach. We work collaboratively with communities, government institutions, civil society organizations, and development partners to design and implement sustainable interventions.

We emphasize local ownership, capacity strengthening, and evidence-based programming to ensure long-term impact and resilience within communities.

Aegis Haven Foundation represents a commitment to protection, inclusion, and opportunity. Inspired by the founder's vision, we strive to create a future where vulnerability does not limit potential, and where every child and person with a disability is supported, protected, and empowered to lead a life of dignity and purpose.
